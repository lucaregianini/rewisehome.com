Compatibility

Every AC
with a remote.

If your air conditioner came with an infrared remote, Rewise Air works with it. Wall units, portables and ceiling cassettes alike.

Built in.

These brands are recognised the moment you press their remote, with temperature, mode, fan and their extras ready to go.

Not on the list? Still works.

Rewise Air learns straight from your remote. Point it at Rewise Air, press each button once, and those buttons live in the app from then on.

What you need

An air conditioner with an infrared remote. Most remotes are: if it has a small dark window at the front, it’s infrared.

Wi-Fi and power

2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, which almost every router provides, and a USB-C charger near the AC.

TVs and lights too

Rewise Air controls infrared TVs from Samsung, LG and Sony, and LED light strips with an infrared remote. Any other infrared device can be taught button by button.

Apps that only work over Wi-Fi from the manufacturer aren’t needed. Air conditioners controlled only by a wired wall panel, with no infrared remote, can’t be controlled.
