  1. 1

    Place it

    On a shelf with a clear view of the AC, 1–6 m away. Infrared window facing it. Plug in USB-C.

  2. 2

    Connect it

    In the app, tap Add a device. It finds Rewise Air over Bluetooth. Pick your Wi-Fi: the password goes only to the device, never to us.

  3. 3

    Press your remote once

    Point it at Rewise Air and press any button. Rewise recognises the brand and sends a test. Not recognised? It learns the buttons from your remote.

  4. 4

Tip: keep the original remote. Both work side by side, and the app keeps up.

If something isn’t working

The AC doesn’t respond

Move Rewise Air so nothing blocks its view of the AC, with the infrared window facing it.

The app can’t find it

Keep your phone close, with Bluetooth on, and check Rewise Air is powered.

Wi-Fi won’t connect

Check the password, and that your router offers 2.4 GHz.

Start over

Remove the device in the app, then add it again. Still stuck? Visit Support or email support@rewisehome.com.
