In short

Rewise makes Rewise Air, the Rewise app, the Rewise server that lets you control your devices away from home, and this website. Rewise keeps only what it needs to run them. It never sells your data or shares it for advertising, the app has no ads or third-party tracking, and this website uses no cookies or analytics.

Rewise is run by Luca Veiga Regianini in Malta, the data controller. Contact: support@rewisehome.com.

Rewise Air and the app

  • Wi-Fi details: your network name and password go from your iPhone to Rewise Air during setup and are stored only on the device, so it can join your network. Rewise never receives them.
  • Air conditioner settings and room readings: power, mode, temperature, fan speed, and the room’s temperature and humidity, so the app can show and change them.
  • Device details: device ID, firmware version, Wi-Fi signal strength, uptime and memory, to find problems and deliver updates.
  • Remote buttons you teach it, and the layout of your controls, so they appear in the app.
  • Energy settings: your AC model or efficiency, your region and your electricity price, used on your iPhone to estimate costs.
  • Bluetooth and local network: used only to find and set up your devices and to control them at home. The app doesn’t use your location.

Without an account, all of this stays between your iPhone and your devices, except what’s described under “The Rewise server” if you use remote control.

Your Rewise account

An account is optional. If you create one, Rewise keeps:

  • Your name and email address. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; Apple then gives us a private relay address.
  • How you sign in: your Apple account identifier, your passkeys’ public keys (the private keys stay in your iCloud Keychain), and, if you set one, your password as a salted one-way hash. We can never see your password.
  • Two-factor authentication, if you turn it on: the authenticator secret, and your recovery codes as one-way hashes.
  • Your profile photo, if you add one, shown to the members of your home.
  • Your home: who’s in it and their role, pending invites, and a copy of your home’s setup (device names and rooms, control layouts, taught buttons, TVs and lights, and the keys your phones use to reach your devices on your home network) so every phone signed in to the home sees the same thing.
  • Sign-in codes: one-time codes sent by email, stored as hashes and deleted once used or after 10 minutes.

The Rewise server

Control away from home, automations and Alexa work through the Rewise server. Your devices keep a secure connection to it. The server keeps:

  • the access tokens of your phones, devices and linked Alexa account, stored only as one-way hashes;
  • the latest state of each device, an activity history of commands (for example “AC set to 22°”, when, and whether it worked), and a history of room readings and AC settings used for charts and energy estimates;
  • your automations and when they last ran;
  • crash reports a device sends after an unexpected restart (where it stopped and its last technical notes, never personal content);
  • standard technical logs (connection times and errors), kept briefly to keep the service running.

The server runs on hardware operated by Rewise in Malta. Your data is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models, and it is not sold.

Amazon Alexa and Siri

If you link Rewise to Alexa, Amazon sends us the commands you give Alexa for your Rewise devices, with a token that identifies your home. We use them only to carry out the command and report the device’s state back. Your voice recordings stay with Amazon under Amazon’s privacy notice; Rewise never receives audio. You can unlink at any time in the Alexa app.

Siri and Shortcuts run on your iPhone; Apple handles Siri requests under its own privacy policy and Rewise receives only the resulting command.

Website sign-ups

If you sign up for a Rewise Air on this website, we keep your name, email address and, if you give it, your country, plus a shortened one-way hash of your internet address to stop abuse. They are emailed to Rewise so we can reply to you about your unit. We use them for nothing else, and delete them once you have your Rewise Air, when you ask, or at the latest 12 months after the last time we were in touch.

How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Command history and room-reading history: up to 13 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: until resolved, and only the latest 200.
  • Website sign-ups: as described above, at most 12 months after last contact.
  • Removing a device in the app deletes its server access and its history.

Legal basis

We process this information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), and crash reports, technical logs and abuse protection to keep it secure and working (legitimate interests). Website sign-ups are handled because you asked us to contact you (consent), which you can withdraw at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its use. Email support@rewisehome.com and we’ll answer within one month. You can delete your account in the app at any time: here’s how. You can also complain to Malta’s Information and Data Protection Commissioner (idpc.org.mt).

Security

Connections are encrypted, tokens and passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, software updates are signed and checked by each device, and you can protect your account with a passkey and two-factor authentication.

Children

Rewise is meant to be set up by adults. It isn’t directed at children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and important changes will be announced in the app.
