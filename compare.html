Choosing a smart AC controller.

Rewise Air, Sensibo, SwitchBot Hub Mini and BroadLink all do the same basic job: they send your air conditioner the signals its infrared remote would, so your phone can control it. Here is what to compare, and what Rewise Air includes.

What Rewise Air includes for €27.99

Which air conditioners does it work with?

Every air conditioner with an infrared remote. Over 40 brands are recognised automatically and any other remote is learned button by button. Check yours on the compatibility page.

How is it set up?

In the Rewise app for iPhone or Android, with no wiring and no changes to the AC. Place it where it can see the AC, plug it into USB-C power, and add it from the app. Read the setup guide.

Does it control the exact settings?

Yes. The app sends the full state (mode, temperature and fan speed) rather than a power toggle. Rewise Air also hears the original remote, so the app stays in step when someone uses it.

Does it measure the room?

Yes. A built-in sensor reads temperature and humidity, and you can use them in automations.

What about voice, Home Assistant and cost?

Siri and Shortcuts, Alexa and Home Assistant are supported. The app shows energy and cost estimates. They are estimates from your AC's rated efficiency, not a meter reading.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every feature is included in the one-off price, with free delivery in Malta, 14-day returns and a two-year guarantee.

What to compare in any smart AC controller

Check the price and any subscription

Some controllers charge a monthly fee for features such as schedules or energy reports. Ask whether each feature you want is included.

Check how it connects to Home Assistant

Some work locally on your network and some go through the maker's cloud. Local control keeps working if the maker's service is down.

Check the sensor

Some have the temperature and humidity sensor built in, some need an extra cable or accessory.

Check Siri and Alexa

Voice control can mean native support or only a Shortcut, so check that it does what you want.

Specifications and prices of other products change. Check each maker's own website for the current details. Order Rewise Air or read the FAQ.
