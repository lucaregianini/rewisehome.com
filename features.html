Features

Everything your AC
was missing.

Rewise Air speaks your air conditioner’s language. The Rewise app turns that into control, comfort and savings.

Control

Your AC, wherever you are.

Temperature, mode and fan speed, from the sofa or from the airport. At home it talks straight over your Wi-Fi, so it keeps working even when the internet is down.

  • Works away from home, on Wi-Fi or mobile data
  • Live room temperature and humidity from a precise sensor
  • Use the normal remote any time: Rewise hears it and the app follows
  • Extra buttons like Turbo, Swing, Light and Quiet, when your AC has them

Remote sync

Keep your remote. Rewise keeps up.

A built-in infrared receiver hears the original remote. Someone turns it to 22° on the sofa, and the app, Siri, Alexa and your automations all know straight away.

Automations

Comfort that runs itself.

Tell Rewise what you want once. It keeps the room right, even when your phone is off.

  • Schedules: weekdays at 6:45, heat to 22°
  • Sleep timer: switch off after you fall asleep
  • React to the room: above 26° at night, start cooling
  • Keep humidity down with Dry mode, automatically

Energy

Know what it costs.

Rewise estimates what your air conditioner uses and costs, from its efficiency rating, the room and how you use it, with electricity prices for your country.

  • Today, the last 7 days and the last 30 days
  • A database of more than 14,000 AC models
  • Your own tariff, or your country’s average price
  • Clearly marked as an estimate, never a meter reading

And there’s more

One puck. The whole room.

Four high-power infrared LEDs give Rewise Air a strong signal and long range, so it reaches your AC, your TV and your lights from one spot on a shelf. No pointing, no extra boxes.

Strong signal, long range

Four infrared LEDs send a strong, wide beam across the whole room, and a built-in receiver hears your remotes. Put it anywhere with a view of the AC and every command lands.

Siri, Shortcuts and Alexa

Ask Siri to cool the bedroom, build your own Shortcuts, or link Alexa and say “Alexa, set the bedroom to 22.”

Accounts and families

Sign in with Apple, a passkey or your email. Invite your family with a link, and every phone sees the same home.

Two ACs, one puck

Two air conditioners in one room? Add the second one in the app. One Rewise Air controls both, each with its own temperature, mode and fan.

TVs and lights

Rewise Air also controls infrared TVs from Samsung, LG and Sony, and LED light strips in the same room.

Teach it any button

Point your remote at Rewise Air and press. That button now lives in the app, in the layout you choose.

Gets better

Signed updates install themselves, checked by the device first.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.

Two-factor
