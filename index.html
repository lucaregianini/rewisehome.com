Compatibility

Every AC with a remote.

If your air conditioner has an infrared remote, Rewise Air works with it. Over 65 AC families are built in, and anything else is learned straight from your own remote.

How it works

Set up in about two minutes.

Place it

Put Rewise Air where it can see your AC, and plug it into USB-C.

Connect it

The Rewise app finds it over Bluetooth and puts it on your Wi-Fi.

Press your remote once

Rewise recognises your AC from its remote and takes it from there.

Features

Your AC, wherever you are.

From anywhere

On the way home.

Turn it on before you arrive, or off when you forgot. Works away from home, not just on your Wi-Fi.

The real room temperature

Not the remote's guess.

A precise sensor reads the room's temperature and humidity, so you know how it actually feels.

Siri and Alexa

Ask Siri or Alexa to cool the bedroom. Alexa support is arriving during the pilot.

Keeps working offline

Control on your home Wi-Fi doesn't depend on the internet.

Stays in sync

Use the normal remote whenever you like; Rewise follows along.

Gets better on its own

Signed software updates arrive automatically, and every update is checked by the device before it installs.

Pilot

The first Rewise Air units go to a small pilot group, with every feature included. Want one, or want to hear when more are ready?
