  4. Object of the declaration: Rewise Air A2 as described above, with firmware 0.3.3 or later.
  5. The object of the declaration described above is in conformity with the relevant Union harmonisation legislation:
    • Directive 2014/53/EU (Radio Equipment), including Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30

    • Radio, Art. 3.2: ETSI EN 300 328 V2.2.2, via the integrated radio module ESP32-C3-WROOM-02 (EU type-examination certificate E1177-210909, Notified Body 1177, TIMCO Engineering, Inc.), used unmodified with its integral PCB antenna.

    • Cybersecurity, Art. 3.3(d)(e): EN 18031-1:2024.
