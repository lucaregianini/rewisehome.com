Setting up

Does it work with my air conditioner?

If it has an infrared remote, yes. Over 40 brands are recognised automatically, and any other remote is learned button by button. Check your brand.

Which phones work?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone with Android 8 or later. Get the Android app.

My Wi-Fi won’t connect.

Rewise Air uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Check the password, and that your router’s 2.4 GHz band is on (most routers combine it with 5 GHz under one name, which is fine). Guest networks that need a sign-in page won’t work.

Where should I put it?

Somewhere with a clear line of sight to the AC, 1–6 metres away, with the window on top facing it. A shelf, a TV cabinet or the top of a wardrobe all work.

Using Rewise

Does it work with Home Assistant?

Yes, Rewise Air A2 does. In HACS, add the Rewise integration as a custom repository, download it and restart Home Assistant. It finds your Rewise Air on its own; then paste the connection code from the Rewise app (your Air A2 → Settings → Home Assistant). Home Assistant controls it directly on your home network.

How do I control it away from home?

Sign in to your Rewise account in the app’s Settings. Remote access switches on by itself, on Wi-Fi or mobile data. At home, the app talks straight to Rewise Air, even without internet.

How do I share it with my family?

Invite them from Settings → Account. Send the link; when they open it and sign in, they see the same home and devices.

How do I set up Alexa?

In the Rewise app, open Alexa in Settings and follow the steps to link the Rewise skill in the Alexa app. Then say “Alexa, discover devices”. Use the name you gave the device, for example “Alexa, set the bedroom to 22”.

Can I still use my normal remote?

Yes. Rewise Air has an infrared receiver, so it hears the original remote and the app, Siri, Alexa and your automations stay up to date.

I have two ACs in the same room. Do I need two?

No. One Rewise Air can control both. Set it up with the first AC, then in the app open the Rewise Air’s settings and choose Add Another AC in This Room. Each AC gets its own controls.

What is Rewise Home?

A plan for more rooms: if you own a Rewise Air, borrow up to 3 more for €9.99 a month on a 6-month plan. Ask about it on the enquiry page and we’ll send the full terms before anything is charged.

Is there a subscription?

No. The app and every feature are included with your Rewise Air: control at home and away, automations, energy estimates, Siri and Alexa.

Can I return it?

Yes. You can return it within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, and it has a two-year guarantee. Email support@rewisehome.com.

Are the energy figures exact?

No, they’re estimates. Rewise works them out from your AC’s rated efficiency, the room readings and the settings it sent. Your electricity bill is always the true number.

What does “Check for missed commands” do?

An optional setting. After a command, Rewise watches the room. If the temperature doesn’t move the way it should, it tells you, in case something blocked the signal.

Account and privacy

Do I need an account?

No. Rewise Air works on your home Wi-Fi without one. An account adds control from anywhere, family sharing, Alexa and the same devices on every phone.

I forgot my password.

Sign in with an email code instead: choose email on the sign-in screen and Rewise sends you a one-time code. If you use two-factor authentication and lost your authenticator, use one of your recovery codes.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. More about deleting your account.

What data does Rewise keep?

Only what’s needed to control your devices. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. Read the Privacy Policy.

Rewise Air

How does it update?

Updates arrive automatically. Each is signed, Rewise Air checks it before installing, and it goes back to the previous version if anything fails.

I found a security problem.

Thank you. Email support@rewisehome.com with “Security” in the subject and what you found. We’ll reply within 3 working days and keep you updated until it’s fixed. Please don’t test against devices or accounts that aren’t yours.

Something’s broken. What about the guarantee?

Rewise Air has a two-year guarantee under EU law. Email support@rewisehome.com and we’ll repair, replace or refund.
