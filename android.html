Good to know

  • Why does Android ask? Rewise isn’t on Google Play yet, so Android checks once that you trust where the app came from.
  • Google Play Protect warning? Tap More details, then Install anyway. It shows for apps that aren’t on Google Play.
  • Can’t find the download? Open the Files app and look in Downloads for Rewise-1.0.0.apk.
  • Next: open Rewise and tap Add a device, or sign in to your Rewise account. The setup guide takes it from there.
  • Updates: download the new version from this page and install it the same way. Your devices and settings stay.
  • Using an iPhone too? Sign in to the same Rewise account on both, and every phone sees the same home.
