How to install the Android app

Then, three steps.

About two minutes, from the box to cool air.

Place it

On a shelf 1–6 m from the AC, with the dark window on top facing it. Plug it into any USB-C phone charger.

Connect it

In the app, tap Add a device and keep your phone close. Choose your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and enter its password.

Press your remote once

Point your AC’s remote at Rewise Air and press any button. Rewise recognises your AC and sends a test.
