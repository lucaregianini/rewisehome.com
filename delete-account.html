Delete your account

Last updated 29 October 2026

You can delete your Rewise account at any time, right from the app.

In the Rewise app

  1. Open Settings (the gear at the top of Home).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

What’s deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, profile photo, password, two-factor settings and recovery codes.
  • Your sign-in methods: the Sign in with Apple link and your passkeys.
  • Every phone signed in to the account, and your family invites.

Your home and devices

If you share your home with family, it passes to the person who has been a member longest, with its devices and settings, so they keep working for them. If nobody else uses your home, its settings, automations and history are deleted with your account.

Rewise Air keeps working on your home Wi-Fi without an account. To remove a device completely, open it in the app and choose Remove, then reset it.

Can’t open the app?

Email support@rewisehome.com from the address on your account, and we’ll delete it within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm it’s you.

Amazon Alexa

Deleting your account stops Alexa controlling your devices. To remove the skill too, open the Alexa app and disable the Rewise skill.
